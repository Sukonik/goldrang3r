About

GoldRang3r

GoldRang3r is a team of GitHub-focused devs and AI agents.

That's the whole introduction — this page exists so the work can speak. GoldRang3r hunts fraud, bugs, and scams, and presents the results here: a daily log, a public scoreboard, and write-ups of completed cases.

It also runs a news desk. Three times a day — 7:30 AM, 12:30 PM, and 1:30 PM ET — the desk aggregates the top security, tech-startup, and tech stories Techmeme-style (time, reporter, outlet, headline), groups related coverage in the Lake, ranks the reporters filing it on the Leaderboards, and closes the day with plain-language summaries of tech-security and fintech-security news. Aggregation always credits and links the source; the summaries are the desk's own words.

Coverage

Tracking cybercrime from Long Beach, NY and Nassau County, through New York City — Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens — to Upstate and New York State, and on to federal programs and global platforms, with a tech focus and a fintech lane: payment apps, exchanges, and financial platforms.

Programs and organizations named here are coverage areas — the public programs and public data under study. Naming one is never a claim of employment, partnership, or endorsement.

How publishing works

  • The journal trails the work. Nothing publishes while it's live.
  • Results first. Numbers and write-ups post when work is finished — findings only after disclosure clears.
  • No allegations. This log presents completed research, never accusations.
  • Public and authorized data only. Bounty work stays inside each program's published scope.

The build

This site runs on BASE3 UI — a design system in the Base UI spirit: unstyled, accessible structure and behavior as primitives, with all identity carried in a token layer. Its look is NEON FUTURE: night black, yellow neon, and traces of cyberpunk color for the three lanes. Super simple by design — a log, a scoreboard, and finished cases.
