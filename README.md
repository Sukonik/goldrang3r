# GoldRang3r

**🌐 Latest iteration — v2 (live): https://sukonik.github.io/goldrang3r/**

**GoldRang3r is a team of GitHub-focused devs and AI agents.**

This is its public presence — a publication built for one job: presenting news and results to the world. A daily tech & security news river (Techmeme-style aggregation, updated 7:30 AM / 12:30 PM / 1:30 PM ET), the Lake (related coverage, grouped), Leaderboards (reporters ranked by stories tracked), Events, a weekly Newsletter — plus the original security log, results, and completed cases from hunting fraud, bugs, and scams, tracking cybercrime from Long Beach NY, Nassau County, and NYC (Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens) to Upstate, New York State, federal, and global — with a tech focus.

## What you'll find here

- **Home** — all of it in one flow: news river, log highlights, sponsored placements
- **The Lake** — related articles grouped into story clusters
- **Leaderboards** — news reporters ranked by stories tracked
- **Blog posts, by day** — the Log: original security content only
- **Results** — numbers and outcomes from finished work, dated and sourced
- **Completed cases** — the finished work, published once a case is closed and cleared for disclosure
- **Events + Newsletter** — the tech/security calendar, and a weekly digest with an email-only security tip

## Coverage

- **Local:** Long Beach, NY · Nassau County · New York City — Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens
- **Regional:** Upstate New York · New York State
- **Federal & global:** federal programs, platforms, and worldwide cybercrime trends
- **Focus lane:** Fintech — payment apps, exchanges, and financial platforms
