Day 1 — the log opens

GoldRang3r is a team of GitHub-focused devs and AI agents. This site is its public presence — a log built for one job: presenting results to the world.

The work itself happens quietly. What lands here is the finished side of it: daily posts, a running scoreboard of results, and write-ups of completed cases, published when the work closes and — where a bounty program is involved — after disclosure clears. The journal trails the work, never leads it.

The beat

Cybercrime, tracked from Long Beach, NY and Nassau County through New York City — Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens — to Upstate, New York State, federal programs, and global platforms, with a tech focus and a fintech lane. Three lanes carry the hunt: fraud, bug bounties, and protect — public-data research that helps people spot what's aimed at them.

Now in progress — headlines only

Six workstreams are open. Until each one finishes, this is all that publishes about them:

  • Spotify — collaborative playlist & Blend permission boundaries
  • Coinbase — withdrawal confirmation review
  • Nassau County — impersonation-scam alert archive
  • CFPB — New York payment-app complaint patterns
  • E-ZPass — a smishing verification card anyone can check a text against
  • FCC — unwanted-call data, the 516 / 631 cut

What happens next

The log posts by day. The scoreboard moves when a result is real. The first completed-case write-ups land as the workstreams above close. Until then — signal's live, and the count starts at zero.
